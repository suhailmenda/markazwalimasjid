Markaz Wali Masjid Mobile App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

Markaz Wali Masjid ("we", "our", "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("Application" or "App") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use the Markaz Wali Masjid App. If you do not agree with any of the practices described in this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use and do not use the Application.

Quick Overview

Markaz Wali Masjid App provides prayer schedules, Azaan alerts, and mosque announcements for the community. We do not require user account registration or personal identity documents for general usage. We do not collect, sell, or rent your personal contact information (e.g., address book, precise GPS location tracking) to any third parties or advertisers.

The App stores your preferences locally on your physical device (using secure local storage / SharedPreferences) to enable functionality offline without logging in.

We use trusted third-party service providers such as Google Firebase (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firestore) for real-time notification dispatch, cloud data synchronization for prayer tables, and broadcast announcements.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • User Preferences: Prayer calculation methods, notification preferences, sound/athan preferences, and subscribed notification channels (e.g., General Announcements, Daily Reminders).
  • Feedback / Support Inquiries: Information you provide when contacting us for support.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and Usage Information: Device type, operating system version, app version, and basic technical logs.
  • Notification Tokens (FCM Tokens): When you enable push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging generates an anonymous device push token to deliver announcements, event updates, and prayer alerts to your device.
  • Local Storage: The App stores your preferences locally on your device to function offline without logging in.

1.3 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • Account Registration: We do not require user account registration or personal identity documents for general usage.
  • Personal Contact Information: We do not collect, sell, or rent your personal contact information (e.g., address book, precise GPS location tracking) to any third parties or advertisers.

2. How We Use Collected Information

  • Providing accurate prayer schedules, Azaan alerts, and mosque announcements.
  • Sending timely push notifications and daily reminders you have subscribed to.
  • Maintaining app stability, diagnosing technical bugs, and improving user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

Our application utilizes trusted third-party service providers that process data in accordance with industry security standards:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Cloud Messaging & Firestore): Used for real-time notification dispatch, cloud data synchronization for prayer tables, and broadcast announcements. (See Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy).

4. Permissions Used by the Application

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / Apple Push Notifications): Required to alert you for prayer times, Azaan, and important mosque announcements.
  • Exact Alarms / Scheduled Notifications: Required for precise local Azaan and Iqamah time alerts even when the device is locked or the app is closed.

5. Data Retention & Security

  • Any locally saved data resides solely on your physical device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the application cache or uninstalling the app.
  • We adopt appropriate security and data collection practices to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of data.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically to collect personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.
